## SDS Workshops 25/26

Welcome! This public repository hosts research projects conducted by the research members of the Statistics and Data Science Society. The projects have been segmented by academic years and you’ll find the relevant code, data and reports for each of the projects undertaken. Viewers are encouraged to watch or star this repo to stay updated as new projects will continue to be added.

### What you’ll find
- **All research folders** organized by Academic Year and Projects
- **Runnable notebooks and scripts** used to run the project
- **Datasets** or links to data required
- **Per-project `README.md`** with relevant information and instructions
- **Reports and Slides** as in the final submission of the members to the society and relevant third parties

## How to navigate
Each project has its own directory with a consistent structure:

```text
(AY X SEM X) PROJECT_NAME
    ├── DATA_FOLDER
    │   └── DATA_SHEETS
    ├── MAIN_CODE_FILE
    ├── OTHER_SCRIPTS
    ├── requirements.txt
    ├── Report.pdf
    └── README.md
```
