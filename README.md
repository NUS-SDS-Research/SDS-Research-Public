# Project Showcase — NUS Statistics & Data Science Society

At NUS SDS, we believe that one of the best ways to learn data science is to apply it to problems that actually matter. The projects below were built entirely by our members this year, combining solid technical foundations with genuine research curiosity.

---

## ESG Analytics for Cryptocurrency Investment

Does ESG actually matter in crypto markets? We wanted to tackle that question, and aimed towards scoring a broad range of cryptocurrencies across environmental, social, and governance criteria, then running statistical tests and machine learning models to see whether those ratings could explain investment outcomes.

The findings were nuanced. The Social pillar proved to be the only consistent positive signal for risk-adjusted returns, while higher ESG classification offered surprisingly little protection during downturns. Machine learning models confirmed that broader market timing effects dominated any ESG signal by a wide margin, suggesting ESG in crypto is better used as a risk lens than a return predictor.

---

## Federated Learning as a Service (FLaaS)

What if two parties could train a shared model without ever sharing their raw data? That is the premise behind Federated Learning, and this project built a working implementation from scratch. Our members developed a full pipeline across incremental stages, from data engineering and model training through to differential privacy, experiment tracking, and a production-grade orchestration layer.

The project tackled real challenges including severe class imbalance in fraud detection, and surfaced a genuinely interesting finding: privacy-preserving noise, typically seen as harmful to model quality, actually improved performance on the fraud dataset by preventing the model from collapsing to a trivial solution.
